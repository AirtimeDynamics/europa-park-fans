Season schedule

Opening hours.

Europa-Park has a summer season and a winter season. Hours change by date, so the official calendar is the final reference.

Summer & Halloween

9 AM – 6 PM+

In the summer and Halloween seasons the park opens at 9 a.m. and closes at 6 p.m. at the earliest, later on many days.

HALLOWinter & winter

11 AM – 7 PM+

From November the park opens at 11 a.m. and stays open until at least 7 p.m. The winter season ends on 9 January 2027.

Next season

From Mar 7, 2027

Pre-Opening Weeks run 7–19 March 2027 and the summer season starts on 20 March. Summer tickets go on sale on 10 November 2026.

Visit strategy

Plan it right.

One section combining the three practical things visitors usually need: when to come, how to plan a family day, and what to pack.

Best time to visit

Use the official calendar to pick a day, then check the hours of the season you are visiting.

Weekdays outside school holidays are usually calmer than weekends.
Arrive before opening and start at the far end of the park instead of the front.
Halloween, HALLOWinter and the winter season change the rhythm of the day.

Family planning

Start with rides that match your group’s height limits, then work outward area by area instead of crossing the park repeatedly.

Check the height requirement of major rides before queueing.
The Kingdom of the Minimoys, Ireland and Grimm’s Enchanted Forest are built for younger riders.
Keep meal, rest and shade breaks in the plan instead of treating them as an afterthought.

What to bring

Pack for a long day on foot, with sun in summer and cold evenings in winter.

Comfortable shoes and clothes you can move in all day.
Sun protection and water in summer, warm layers in winter.
Leave loose items with a non-rider or in a locker: some coasters do not allow them in the queue.
How it grew

50 years of
Europe.

From a 16-hectare park in 1975 to the eleven-time best theme park in the world: the milestones that shaped Europa-Park.

1975
1975Grand opening

Europa-Park opens

The Mack family opens the park in Rust on 12 July 1975, on 16 hectares.

1982
1982First country

Italy

Italy becomes the first country-themed area and sets the template for the whole park.

1989
1989Indoor coaster

Eurosat

Eurosat opens inside the silver sphere; it returns in 2018 as Eurosat – CanCan Coaster.

1995
1995Resort

Hotel El Andaluz

El Andaluz opens as the first hotel in a German theme park.

2001
2001Winter season

Open in winter

The park runs its first winter season, with an ice rink and a circus revue.

2002
2002Hyper coaster

Silver Star

Silver Star opens as Europe’s tallest and fastest roller coaster at the time.

2009
2009Iceland

Blue Fire Megacoaster

Iceland opens with Blue Fire, the first ride to turn Europa-Park upside down.

2012
2012Wooden giant

Wodan Timburcoaster

Wodan opens as the park’s first giant wooden roller coaster.

2014
2014Minimoys

Arthur

The Kingdom of the Minimoys opens with the inverted family coaster Arthur.

2018
2018Fire & reboot

A difficult year

A fire in May destroys Pirates in Batavia and parts of Scandinavia; in September, Eurosat reopens as the CanCan Coaster.

2019
2019Water park

Rulantica

The Rulantica indoor water park opens next door on 28 November.

2024
2024Croatia

Voltron Nevera

Croatia opens with Voltron Nevera, the Golden Ticket winner for Best New Roller Coaster; Alpenexpress Enzian returns after the 2023 fire.

2026
2026Monaco

Monaco & an 11th Golden Ticket

Silver Star’s plaza becomes Monaco, the park partners with ESA, and it is named Best Theme Park in the World for the eleventh time.

2028
2028Announced

ESA space area

A space-themed area with a new Mack family coaster, created with ESA, is due to open on the former Euro-Mir site by 2028.
